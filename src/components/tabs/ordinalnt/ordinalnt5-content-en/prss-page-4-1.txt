RAW:: 1. For any \(A \in P\), \(\textrm{expand}(\textrm{gen}(A,E),n) = A\).
2. For any \((A,B) \in P^2\),
\(\textrm{expand}(\textrm{gen}(A,\textrm{gen}(B,E)),n) = A \frown \underbrace{(0) \frown B^+ \frown \cdots \frown (0) \frown B^+}_n\).
3. For any \((A,B,C) \in P^2 \times (P \setminus \{E\})\),
\(\textrm{expand}(\textrm{gen}(A,\textrm{gen}(B,C)),n) = \textrm{gen}(A,\textrm{expand}(\textrm{gen}(B,C),n))\).
