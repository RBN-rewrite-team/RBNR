以下是\(\psi_M\)函数的例子
\[\psi_M(M)=I\]
\[\psi_M(M+1)=\Omega_{I+1}\]
\[\psi_M(M2)=I_2\]
\[\psi_M(M3)=I_3\]
\[\psi_M(M\Omega)=I_\Omega\]
\[\psi_M(M^2)=I(1,0)\]
\[\psi_M(M^2+M)=I_{I(1,0)+1}\]
\[\psi_M(M^22)=I(1,1)\]
\[\psi_M(M^3)=I(2,0)\]
\[\psi_M(M^\omega)=I(\omega,0)\]
\[\psi_M(M^M)=I(1,0,0)\]
\[\psi_M(M^{M^2})=I(1,0,0,0)\]
\[\psi_M(M^{M^M})=I(1@(1,0))\]
\[\psi_M(\varepsilon_{M+1})=\sup\{I,I(1,0),I(1@(1,0)),I(1@(1@(1,0))),I(1@(1@(1@(1,0)))),\cdots\}\]
