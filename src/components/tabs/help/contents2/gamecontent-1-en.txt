This game was coded by three individuals: VeyrrDefine, 0100000000a7, and Seanxlx.
Some game content design was completed by these three and others (details available on the “About the Game” page).
Internal disagreements within the development team are common, so certain mechanics may change.
Due to certain individuals' actions, the game will force you to watch cutscenes.

You start the game with 0 Number. You can increase your Number by clicking the Successor button.
You can purchase upgrades by clicking U0-1, U0-2, or U0-3 below. These upgrades consume Number.
B0-1 is a special upgrade. You can repeatedly click to purchase it, with its price increasing each time.

B0-1 automates the Successor button, which proves useful later.

Upon reaching 1000 Number, you can purchase U0-3 to unlock the Addition Layer.
